      Hugo M. Santos

      PhD, MRSC · Principal Investigator & Facility Director

      Hugo trained in proteomics through a joint PhD between NOVA Lisbon and the Turku Centre for Biotechnology, with postdoctoral work at the University of Vigo and at the Institute for Biomedicine and Biotechnology in Barcelona. He joined NOVA FCT in 2013, won an FCT Investigator Starting Grant in 2016 and founded the Proteomass facility the same year. He also works with the University of Pittsburgh to standardise high-resolution MS in cancer research.
